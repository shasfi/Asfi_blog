North Carolina Woman Vanishes After Swim on Grenada Beach Vacation

A North Carolina woman, Elizabeth Waddell, has gone missing after a swim at Grand Anse Beach in Grenada during her vacation. The incident has sparked widespread concern, with family, friends, and authorities intensifying efforts to locate her.

What Happened?

Elizabeth Waddell, a physical therapist from Cary, North Carolina, was on a Caribbean getaway when she disappeared. According to reports, she left her Airbnb accommodation to go for a swim at Grand Anse Beach, one of Grenada’s most popular tourist destinations. She was last seen entering the water, and her whereabouts remain unknown.

Family and Friends Rally for Answers

Her family and friends have been vocal about their concerns, urging the public and authorities to assist in the search. Statements from her loved ones describe her as a devoted mother and wife, making her sudden disappearance even more alarming.

Search Efforts Intensify

Local authorities in Grenada, along with U.S. officials, have launched a comprehensive search operation. Divers, helicopters, and ground teams are scouring the area for any signs of Elizabeth. The investigation is ongoing, with no concrete leads as of yet.

Grand Anse Beach: A Tourist Hotspot

Grand Anse Beach is known for its pristine waters and picturesque views, attracting thousands of tourists annually. However, the disappearance has cast a shadow over the idyllic location, raising questions about safety measures for visitors.

What’s Next?

As the search continues, Elizabeth’s family remains hopeful for her safe return. They have appealed to anyone with information to come forward, emphasizing that every detail could be crucial in solving this mystery.

Conclusion: The Core Question Answered

Elizabeth Waddell, a North Carolina woman, vanished after swimming at Grand Anse Beach in Grenada. Despite extensive search efforts, her whereabouts remain unknown, and her family continues to seek answers.
