Houthis Claim Missile Strike on Riyadh as Explosions Echo in Capital

Houthies Claim Responsibility for Missile Strike on Riyadh

Explosions were heard in the Saudi capital Riyadh late on Sunday night, with Yemen's Houthi movement claiming responsibility for what they described as a missile strike targeting the city. The group said the attack was carried out using a ballistic missile, marking another escalation in a conflict that has increasingly drawn in regional powers and civilian infrastructure.

The Houthis, who control much of northern Yemen, have been engaged in a protracted war with a Saudi-led coalition since 2015. In recent months, their ability to project force beyond Yemen’s borders has grown, with repeated drone and missile attacks on key Saudi cities including Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. These strikes have targeted airports, military installations, and energy facilities, raising fears about the widening scope of the conflict.

What Exactly Happened in Riyadh?

While details remain limited, local media reported that the explosions occurred in the early hours of Monday morning. No immediate casualties were confirmed by Saudi authorities at the time of writing, though emergency services were dispatched to several locations across the capital. The Houthis claimed the attack struck a high-value target within Riyadh, though they did not specify which facility.

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Interior has not yet issued an official statement regarding the incident. However, sources familiar with security matters told Euronews that debris from a missile or drone was recovered in the area, suggesting the Houthis successfully penetrated Saudi air defenses.

Context: Escalating Houthi Attacks on Saudi Arabia

This latest strike comes amid a surge in cross-border hostilities. Earlier this month, the Houthis launched a series of attacks on Saudi airports, killing at least three people and injuring dozens more, according to reports from Time Magazine and Bloomberg. These incidents represent some of the deadliest operations conducted by the group against Saudi soil.

Reuters reported that one of those attacks specifically targeted Riyadh’s international airport using a ballistic missile. That assault disrupted flights temporarily and underscored the growing reach of Houthi weaponry. Since then, similar incidents have occurred near Jeddah and Medina, prompting heightened alert levels among Saudi security forces.

Why Are the Houthis Targeting Saudi Cities?

The Houthis frame their actions as retaliatory measures against what they view as Saudi aggression in Yemen. They accuse Riyadh of conducting a brutal campaign aimed at restoring the internationally recognized government of President Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi, whom the Houthis overthrew in 2014. From their perspective, striking deep into Saudi territory serves both strategic and symbolic purposes.

Strategically, these attacks aim to pressure Saudi Arabia politically and economically by destabilizing major urban centers. Symbolically, they signal that no part of the kingdom is beyond the Houthis' operational reach. This messaging resonates domestically within Yemen, where anti-Saudi sentiment remains strong among many supporters of the movement.

International Reactions and Regional Implications

The international community has expressed concern over the increasing frequency and severity of Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. The United Nations special envoy for Yemen condemned the latest incident, calling for restraint on all sides and urging renewed diplomatic engagement to de-escalate tensions.

The United States, which previously provided logistical support to the Saudi-led coalition but has since scaled back involvement, issued a statement urging all parties to avoid actions that could further endanger innocent lives. European Union foreign policy chief Josep Borrell also called for urgent talks between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis to prevent a broader regional war.

Impact on Civilian Populations

Beyond the immediate threat posed by explosions and gunfire, these escalating attacks have taken a toll on civilians living in affected areas. In Riyadh alone, residents have reported disruptions to daily routines, increased anxiety, and difficulties accessing essential services during periods of heightened alert. Schools and universities have implemented contingency plans, while hospitals have activated emergency protocols to handle potential influxes of patients.

For ordinary Saudis, the reality of living under constant threat adds another layer of complexity to an already challenging situation. Many describe feeling caught in the middle of a conflict they did not choose but must endure nonetheless. Mental health professionals warn that prolonged exposure to such stressors may lead to long-term psychological impacts, especially among children and elderly populations.

Broader Geopolitical Ramifications

As the conflict continues to evolve, its implications extend far beyond the borders of Yemen and Saudi Arabia. Neighboring countries like the UAE, Kuwait, and Bahrain find themselves drawn into the orbit of this regional instability. Meanwhile, global powers including China and Russia watch closely, weighing opportunities to expand influence through strategic partnerships or humanitarian aid initiatives.

Oil markets have shown signs of volatility following each new wave of attacks, reflecting investor unease over supply chain risks associated with disruptions in the Persian Gulf region. Analysts suggest that continued escalation could prompt shifts in energy pricing dynamics, potentially affecting economies worldwide dependent on Middle Eastern crude exports.

At the same time, humanitarian crises continue unabated inside Yemen itself. According to UN agencies operating there, millions of people lack access to basic necessities such as food, clean water, and medical care. Disease outbreaks pose additional threats, particularly in regions controlled by opposing factions where coordination proves difficult despite international efforts to facilitate delivery of aid supplies.

Diplomatic Prospects Amid Ongoing Tensions

Despite mounting concerns, prospects for meaningful dialogue appear dim. Previous attempts at negotiation—most notably those mediated by the United Nations—have stalled due to mutual mistrust and divergent political objectives. While some stakeholders advocate for renewed peace talks focused on ceasefire agreements and prisoner exchanges, others argue that military pressure remains necessary until core grievances are addressed satisfactorily.

In Washington, debates rage over whether renewed U.S. involvement might help broker a resolution. Critics caution against becoming entangled once again in costly interventions lacking clear exit strategies. Supporters counter that disengagement allows hostile actors to gain ground unchecked, ultimately threatening American interests throughout the region.

Looking Ahead: What Comes Next?

With no signs of de-escalation emerging anytime soon, observers expect more turbulence ahead. Both sides possess capabilities sufficient to inflict significant damage if hostilities intensify. Whether cooler heads prevail will likely depend on calculated assessments made behind closed doors rather than public posturing alone.

For now, citizens on both sides brace themselves for whatever unfolds next. Their resilience reflects a shared desire for stability even amid chaos—an enduring testament to humanity’s capacity to endure hardship when faced with uncertainty.

Direct Answer: Who Is Responsible for the Recent Explosions in Riyadh?

Yemen’s Houthi movement claimed responsibility for the recent explosions heard in Riyadh, stating they carried out a missile strike targeting the Saudi capital. While Saudi officials have not officially confirmed details, preliminary assessments indicate the Houthis successfully penetrated air defenses, continuing a pattern of escalating cross-border attacks amid the ongoing Yemen conflict.
